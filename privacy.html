BAVSA Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

BAVSA is a free study app for high school students ages 13 and up, made by [your name]. This page explains what BAVSA stores and why. Questions? Email [your email].

Who can use BAVSA

BAVSA is for students 13 and older. If you're under 13, please don't use it. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete their data.

What we store

  • Your Google sign-in (only if you sign in for class chats): your name, email and profile photo come from Google. Classmates see only your first name and photo.
  • Your profile: your first name, grade, school, classes, goals, languages, study plans and settings. Only you can see these.
  • Class chat messages you post, which everyone at your school in that class can see. You can delete your own messages.
  • Reports and contact messages you send, which only BAVSA's maker can read.

This information is stored with Google Firebase. Some settings are also saved on your own device.

Bavi, the AI tutor

Bavi is an AI, not a person, and it can make mistakes. By default, Bavi runs on your own phone: the first time you use it, your browser downloads a small open-source AI model, and after that your questions and photos are answered on your device and aren't sent to us.

If you turn on Smarter answers in Settings, what you type or photograph for Bavi is sent to an AI model through your own Puter account (puter.com). Puter's privacy policy and terms cover that account. Either way, BAVSA doesn't keep a copy of your Bavi conversations on a server.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your information or show ads.
  • We don't share your information except as needed to run the services above, or when the law requires it.

Staying safe

Class chats block phone numbers, emails, social handles and offensive words. Anyone can report or block a classmate. Reported messages are reviewed and people who break the rules are removed. If someone is in danger, tell a trusted adult or call 911.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings and tap Delete my BAVSA data, or email [your email] and we'll delete your account information.
